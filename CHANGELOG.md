# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Manage customers, opportunities, products, price lists, quotations, and sales orders.
  Quotations and orders keep revision history and can be exported as PDFs.
- Track stock, purchasing, deliveries, production orders, work centres, routings, and
  supplier invoice matching.
- Handle helpdesk tickets, customer portal requests, repairs, and field jobs. Helpdesk includes
  SLA tracking, reply templates, checklists, and ticket bundles.
- Use dark mode, global search, saved filters, activity panels, and Kanban boards for sales
  opportunities and helpdesk tickets in the Flutter app.
- An optional MCP pilot offers a read-only order lookup for a consenting user and one company.
  It requires a compatible OAuth issuer.
